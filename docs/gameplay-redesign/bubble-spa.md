# Bubble Spa — Style the doll with suds

Bring the Dress Up doll close to the mirror. Give her a ridiculous bubble hairstyle with both the scale and the squish of a handful of shaving foam. The mirror keeps her whole silhouette visible while your hand works.

## A first round

Press on her hair to grow a mound. Pull its tip sideways and it curls into a horn. Make the other side taller, then tap the doll's nose. She sneezes. Both horns wobble, the tall one folds over her eye, and she peeks out. Pinch off the fallen curl with a tap or sweep it back up.

## What brings you back

Style a cloud wig, curled moustache or beard. The foam sticks where you leave it. A duck-shaped shower gives you a broad rinse exactly where you stroke, so you can trim one side and keep the rest. Squeeze it over her head for a full rinse and a fresh face in the mirror. Picture prompts such as bunny ears are optional ideas to interpret freely.

## Smallest playable version

Prototype white foam, one tint, the mirror, sneeze and rinse. Give the foam a stable base and wobbly tips, and let an upward stroke stretch any nearby tuft. The first test is whether a child can make two recognizably different silhouettes and recognize her own creation after the doll shakes her head.
