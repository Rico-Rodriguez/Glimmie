# Aquarium — Build a reef and watch who moves in

Build little homes for fish. The fish tell you what works through their behavior. A shy clownfish peeks from cover, tetras want room to school, and a moray's eyes appear inside a cave you made.

## A first round

Start with a clownfish hovering beside a single rock. Slide a waving anemone onto the sand. The fish darts into its tentacles and peeks out. Scatter a pinch of food to coax it out, then watch it return home. Move the anemone across the tank and the fish makes the trip with it.

## What brings you back

Place a flat rock across two stones. As soon as there is a usable opening, two eyes blink underneath. Widen it and the moray pokes its head out. Build a second arch nearby and the eel swims between them. A school threads through open gaps, splitting around obstacles and regrouping. Every rearrangement changes the little scene you get to watch.

## Smallest playable version

Prototype one tank, three rock shapes, one anemone and two residents. Give rocks broad snap points and show a faint cave silhouette as a slab nears two supports. A misplaced rock remains movable with one finger. Start the playtest with the loose slab beside two supports. Watch for a child moving a piece just to see how an animal changes its behavior.
