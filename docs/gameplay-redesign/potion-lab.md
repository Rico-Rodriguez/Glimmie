# Potion Lab — Invent spells and play with them

Put an enchanted play table beside the cauldron. Each ingredient gives an object one physical property. The experiment begins when you pour the potion and get your hands on the result.

## A first round

A wooden frog sits beside the flask. Drop in a bubble berry, swirl once, then dab the brew on the frog. It floats. Push it across the table and it bumps the flask. Add a spring bean to the same brew and dab it again. Now the frog floats until you pull it down and release it. It springs upward and rings the bell hanging above the table.

## What brings you back

Try the same brew on a handful of wooden stars. One swipe sends a whole constellation bobbing around the room. Or swap the berry for a heavy pebble. The stars fall, bounce low and rattle across the table. Ingredient pictures on the bottle let you remake a favorite. A water drop restores any object immediately.

## Smallest playable version

Prototype three ingredients (float, bounce and heavy), the frog, the stars and a bell. Use two ingredient slots. Float and heavy cancel to ordinary weight, while bounce combines with either. Show the current mix directly on the bottle. Test whether a child can make the frog reach the bell, then choose a different object to try the same spell on without a prompt.
